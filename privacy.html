Kera Jobs Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Kera Jobs is an independent government job information application. It is not affiliated with any government organization.

1. Information We Process

Kera Jobs may store job preferences, saved jobs, and app settings locally on your device.

2. Advertising

Kera Jobs uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, app interactions, and other data according to their applicable policies and consent requirements.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

3. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Kera Jobs requests advertising consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. Users can manage their available privacy choices through the consent interface.

4. Job Information

The app provides job information and links to external recruitment websites. External websites have their own privacy policies.

5. Data Storage

Job preferences, saved jobs and cached vacancy information may be stored locally on the device. The app may retrieve vacancy information from online sources.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the application. No system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Kera Jobs is intended for users seeking employment. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our own forms.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when application features or data practices change.
